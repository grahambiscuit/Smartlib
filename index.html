What's Inside

Everything a library
needs to thrive.

Purpose-built features for both students and administrators — designed to eliminate friction and keep books moving.

📖
Smart Book Browser

Search, filter by genre and status, and discover books with a fuzzy search engine that finds matches even with typos.

📬
Borrow & Return Requests

Students send borrow and return requests; admins approve with a single click. Full workflow, fully tracked.

🔔
In-App Notifications

Admins send custom messages or use quick templates for overdue reminders, return requests, and welcomes.

📊
Admin Dashboard

Live stats for total books, availability, student count, online users, and pending requests — all at a glance.

🕓
Borrowing History

Students get a personal history log of every book borrowed, when it was taken, and when it was returned.

🌙
Dark & Light Themes

Comfortable reading in any environment. Theme preference is saved across sessions automatically.
